# Edinburgh Festival Fringe - Saturday Schedule

A comprehensive, chronological itinerary of your curated Fringe shows for this Saturday, including performance times, venues, and brief descriptions.

### 🕒 10:50 - 11:50 | Absolute Bus Stops of Northern Europe and Beyond
* **Venue:** Paradise in Augustines
* **Genre:** Multimedia Comedy
* **Synopsis:** A quirky, lighthearted slideshow lecture celebrating the hidden magic and obscure rating systems of European bus shelters.
